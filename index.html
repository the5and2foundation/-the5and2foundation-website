The 5&2 Foundation is a Christian nonprofit organization named after one of the most powerful moments in all of Scripture — the miracle of the five loaves and two fish.

We believe that when ordinary people offer what little they have, God multiplies it into something extraordinary. That is not just our name. That is our operating belief and the foundation of everything we do.

We are organized exclusively for charitable and religious purposes, partnering with churches, organizations, and volunteers to demonstrate the love of Jesus through practical service — meeting real needs in real communities.

Our programs are simple and direct: feed the hungry, clothe the vulnerable, provide financial relief in crisis, and offer the spiritual encouragement that sustains the whole person.

Why "5&2"? In John 6, a young boy offered what he had — five loaves and two fish. It seemed impossibly small. Jesus gave thanks, broke it, and fed five thousand with twelve baskets remaining. We exist because we believe the same God still multiplies.

Seasonal clothing drives and hygiene kits for individuals and families — because dignity matters as much as provision.

  • Back-to-school clothing drives
  • Winter coat & blanket distributions
  • Hygiene kit assembly & distribution
  • Personal care essentials for women & children

Financial Assistance

Direct financial relief for rent, utilities, transportation, and crisis needs. We believe in closing the gap between crisis and stability.

  • Referrals to additional resources
